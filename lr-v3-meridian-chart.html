Liver Channel — Five Shu Points

Click a highlighted point. The Liver’s five Shu points run from the big toe to the medial knee: LR1 (Well-Wood), LR2 (Spring-Fire), LR3 (Stream-Earth), LR4 (River-Metal) and LR8 (Sea-Water) — the Yin-channel cycle Wood → Fire → Earth → Metal → Water. The list skips LR5–LR7 (LR5 is Luo-connecting, LR6 is Xi-Cleft). The plate shows the channel in two views: the foot on the left, the medial knee on the right.
