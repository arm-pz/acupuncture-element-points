Conception Vessel (Ren Mai) — Five Element Points

The Conception Vessel courses along the anterior midline of the body, governing all Yin channels. Plotted along its vertical midline over the anatomical vector torso are its five vital Element Points: Wood (CV7), Fire (CV5), Earth (CV13), Air/Metal (CV9), and Water (CV6).

Vessel Origin · 起穴
CV1 Huiyin 會陰

At the perineum, midway between the anus and posterior scrotal commissure / frenulum of labia majora.

↳ Internal pelvic origin from the Uterus (Bao) / Lower Dantian
Vessel Terminus · 止穴
CV24 Chengjiang 承漧

In the mentolabial groove, in the depression below the midpoint of the lower lip.

↳ Internal ascending branches encircle lips, enter gingiva and connect with ST4 / ST1
How It Travels · Anatomical Route Waypoints
Sequential course along the anterior median line (Lineae Alba)
Stage 01 Pelvic Genesis & Pubic Ascent

Originates in the lower pelvic cavity (uterus/Bao), emerges superficially at CV1 Huiyin in the perineum, ascends anteriorly through the pubic hair to CV2 Qugu at the superior margin of the pubic symphysis, passing through CV3 Zhongji and CV4 Guanyuan.

Stage 02 Lower Abdominal Element Gates

Ascends along the lower abdominal midline through vital element points: CV5 Shimen (Fire, 2.5 cun below navel), CV6 Qihai (Water, 1.5 cun below navel), and CV7 Yinjiao (Wood, 1.0 cun below navel), reaching CV8 Shenque in the center of the umbilicus.

Stage 03 Epigastric Cavities & Sternal Course

Continues upward from the navel pit through CV9 Shuifen (Metal/Air, 1.0 cun above), traverses CV12 Zhongwan, reaches CV13 Shangwan (Earth, 5.0 cun above navel), and ascends the sternal midline across CV17 Danzhong (Pericardium Front-Mu) to the throat.

Stage 04 Laryngeal Ascent & Mentolabial Lip

Traverses the suprasternal fossa at CV22 Tiantu, climbs through the larynx and hyoid bone at CV23 Lianquan, reaches the chin, and terminates in the mentolabial groove depression at CV24 Chengjiang below the lower lip.
