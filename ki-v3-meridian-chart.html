Kidney Channel — Five Shu Points

Click a highlighted point. The Kidney’s five Shu points run from the sole to the knee: KI1 (Well-Wood), KI2 (Spring-Fire), KI3 (Stream-Earth), KI7 (River-Metal) and KI10 (Sea-Water) — the Yin-channel cycle Wood → Fire → Earth → Metal → Water, skipping KI4 (Luo point) and KI5 (Xi-Cleft point). The plate shows the channel in two views: the medial leg on the left, the sole on the right.
