Meridian Pathway & Travel 11 Points Total
Start LU1 Zhongfu Anterior-lateral chest wall, 1st intercostal space, 6 cun lateral to the anterior midline
End LU11 Shaoshang Radial side of the thumb, 0.1 cun posterior to the corner of the nail

How it travels: Originates in the Middle Burner (stomach region), descends to connect with the Large Intestine, loops upward past the cardiac orifice, traverses the lungs, and ascends to the throat. It emerges antero-laterally at LU1 below the clavicle, runs down the antero-lateral aspect of the upper arm lateral to the biceps, crosses the cubital fossa (LU5), descends the radial forearm to the radial styloid and wrist crease (LU9), passes the thenar eminence (LU10), and terminates at the radial nail corner of the thumb at LU11. An internal branch separates at LU7 and travels directly to the radial index fingertip to connect with LI1.
