Gallbladder Channel — Five Shu Points

Click a highlighted point. The Gallbladder’s five Shu points run from the fourth toe up the lateral leg to the fibula head: GB44 (Well-Metal), GB43 (Spring-Water), GB41 (Stream-Wood), GB38 (River-Fire) and GB34 (Sea-Earth) — the Yang-channel cycle Metal → Water → Wood → Fire → Earth. The list skips GB42 (no Shu category) and GB40 (Yuan-Source only). The plate shows the channel in two views: the foot on the left, the lateral leg on the right.
