He-Sea · 合 Earth Stomach · Yang

He-Sea point

Landmark

3 cun below ST35, one finger-breadth lateral to the anterior crest of the tibia.

Click point to jump Inspect on Chart →

Yin runs Wood → Fire → Earth → Metal → Water·Yang runs Metal → Water → Wood → Fire → Earth. Both cycles skip the Luo-Connecting and Xi-Cleft points.

Every route runs Well → Sea, from the digits to just below the elbow or knee. The eight interactive overlay channels are LI, HT, SP, ST, LR, GB, KI and BL; LU, SI, PC and TB open the earlier chart format.

Point categories were checked against the classical 五輸穴 tables and nalaayan.com’s per-point badges. Point-location charts are user-supplied; licensing review is pending before any public release.
