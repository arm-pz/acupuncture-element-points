Spleen Channel — Five Shu Points

Click a highlighted point. The Spleen’s five Shu points run from the big toe to the knee: SP1 (Well-Wood), SP2 (Spring-Fire), SP3 (Stream-Earth), SP5 (River-Metal) and SP9 (Sea-Water) — the Yin-channel cycle Wood → Fire → Earth → Metal → Water. The plate shows the channel in two views: the lower leg on the left, the foot on the right.
