# Brief: one-forest-redesign
Date: 2026-11-13

Recorded from the owner's review of the design mocks (not through /brief; the answers below are the owner's requests in their words, condensed).

## 1. What done looks like
The home screen and the point screens feel like one app. Keep the five-elements animated opening and improve it. On a channel page the chart is visible straight away instead of after a wall of writing. Every point shows its own location image together with its location text, because the charts are not to scale. Works on a phone and on desktop. Assets are not touched.

## 2. Guess at the fix or design
The approved mock: the element scene stays behind every screen; tabs move to a bottom dock on phones; each channel page gets a one-line to-scale summary of the channel, a plate card that turns over to the point's location diagram, and a details sheet with the route and relations folded away.

## 3. How we will know if it worked
On a 390x844 phone the plate is fully on the first screen of a channel page, tapping a point shows its diagram with the location text still in view, and the Playwright suite passes.
